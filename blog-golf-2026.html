A Swing Towards Legacy: Edgars Kicks Off 80th Anniversary Celebrations on the Greens

04 September 2026
  • Edgars
  • Golf 2026
  • Bulawayo Country Club
  • 80th Anniversary

The Edgars 80th Anniversary celebrations got off to a memorable start at Bulawayo Country Club with the Edgars 2026 Golf Day.

Held under the theme “A Swing Towards Legacy,” the day brought together valued stakeholders and guests for great golf, meaningful connections and memorable moments.

More than a day on the course, it was a celebration of the relationships and partnerships that have been part of the Edgars story across eight remarkable decades.

It was the perfect opening to a milestone celebration — and the day was only getting started.

80 years of legacy, celebrated one swing at a time.
