Edgars Brings Smiles to Children at Alpha Cottages in Masvingo

09 September 2026
  • CSR
  • Edgars
  • Alpha Cottages
  • Masvingo
  • Donation

A simple gesture of care can make a lasting difference. On 9 September 2026, Edgars Stores Limited visited Alpha Cottages Children’s Home in Masvingo, where the team donated clothing items to support children living at the home.

The donation included jerseys, jackets, tracksuits, shorts and other clothing items that will assist the children with their everyday clothing needs throughout the year.

Alpha Cottages Children’s Home provides care and support to 46 orphaned and vulnerable children between the ages of 2 and 18 years. The children come from different backgrounds and are supported through their education journey, with some attending day schools, boarding schools, polytechnics and vocational training programmes.

Speaking during the visit, Edgars representative the Marketing and Corporate Affairs Executive Mrs Justina Machinya shared that giving back to communities remains an important part of the Edgars journey.

“Edgars has a Corporate Social Responsibility programme where we give back to the community. We do it with love because the Zimbabwean community has supported us, and we are happy for the opportunity to contribute towards these children.”
— Mrs Justina Machinya, Marketing and Corporate Affairs Executive, Edgars Stores Limited

She added that the company values partnerships that create meaningful change.

“We will continue supporting the good work being done at Alpha Cottages. The work you are doing is important, and we are grateful to be part of it,” she said.

Caretaker Superintendent Mrs Chishamiso Lidia Mhizha welcomed Edgars and thanked the company for the donation.

She shared that the home supports children who have experienced different challenges, including abandonment, loss of parents, family difficulties and circumstances where parents are unable to provide care.

“This donation will help our children for a long time as Edgars clothes are of high quality. We thank the CEO, management and everyone at Edgars for remembering us and supporting the children.”
— Mrs Chishamiso Lidia Mhizha, Caretaker Superintendent, Alpha Cottages Children’s Home

The visit highlighted the importance of community partnerships and Edgars’ continued commitment to making a positive difference in the lives of Zimbabweans.
